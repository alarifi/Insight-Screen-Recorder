# Support and feedback

Use this repository's Issues tab to report an issue, suggest a feature or rate your experience. No support email or website has been invented for this kit.

For a useful report, include the app version, Windows version, selected screen/audio device, whether System sound/Microphone were ON, the steps taken and the expected result.

## No microphone sound

Check the headset connection and physical mute switch, select the intended microphone and use Test. A moving meter is stronger evidence than an enabled ON button. If Test receives no speech, compare with Windows' input test or another recorder. Do not assume increasing gain can fix a disconnected or incorrectly routed input.

## Freeze or interrupted recording

This remains a known blocker on the development computer. Avoid long or important recording sessions until stability is established. Include approximate event time and whether camera, system audio and microphone were enabled. Do not post memory dumps or private diagnostics publicly without reviewing their contents.

## Privacy

Issues and attachments in a public repository are public. Remove license keys, personal paths, email addresses and confidential screen/audio content. Do not upload full recordings merely to demonstrate a setting; use the smallest reviewed example needed.
